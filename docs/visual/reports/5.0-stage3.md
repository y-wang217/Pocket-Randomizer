## What was built, by ruling

- **D66, the face is the mark.** `renderRewardCard` lost the kind label, the name,
  the effect line and the note on every kind.
  - An item keeps its sprite and boosted type chip, as since M5.1.
  - A relic is its manifest icon (a lettered placeholder until 5.0/5), and the name
    is on the long press. D36's recorded deviation is retired.
  - Coins are `+N` beside the currency glyph. The word, what coins buy, and the
    balance (QA-003) are the new `coins:` panel, with the balance on `data-detail`.
  - A restore is `+N%` beside a neutral bar filled to N. The title (*Full restore*,
    *Restore 40%*, from one function now, `restoreTitle`) and what it restores are
    the new `restore:` panel.
- **D65.** The relic card carries `capabilityGlyph`, the map node's own mark,
  without the band chevron: the chevron is where the run stands, and a card is not
  the run.
- **D67 and D71.** The three move kinds mount the move card and nothing else. No
  disc. An offer holding any move card stacks all three cards, so siblings stay
  identical in size (Stage V0); an offer of mark-faced cards alone sits three across.
- **D69, tap selects and the band claims.** `renderOfferCards` in
  `screens/reward.ts`:
  - no card has `aria-pressed="true"` at rest;
  - a tap selects the card and opens `ui/band.ts` with an inert copy of it as the
    content, since the band's dim covers the row;
  - *Take* claims; *Back* clears the selection and returns focus to the card.
  - The shop already selected before committing. Its *Buy N and leave* now opens
    the same band with the basket's cards; *Back* keeps the basket. Leaving with
    nothing is not a buy and does not ask.
  - The band has six call sites now, the four of Rev 1 plus these two.
- **D68 and D70.** No new route; the capture card is untouched.
- **The currency family is drawn** for the first time (ruled under D54): `currency-coin`,
  a stack of three coins, through `glyphNode`, so R7's exposure labels count it. The
  first drawing, a slotted disc, read as an info mark at 16px and was redrawn before
  commit. `GLYPH_FAMILIES` is 13; both files it touched are outside `contentHash`.

## Known gaps

- **A reload between a claim and a wild node's capture answer asks the card again.**
  The reward is written to the log when the node completes, after the capture, so
  until then it is only in memory (`core/run.ts`, the `reward` record after Part A,
  and QA-006's replay). Nothing is duplicated, since the log has not got it, but the
  player is asked twice. This predates 5.0/3. Fixing it moves where `core/` records
  the pick, which is a run log question and outside a presentation stage.
- **The shop's wallet still reads *Carrying*, *Basket*, *Left*** without the
  currency glyph. Section 3's coin row names the wallet; it is left for 5.0/4, which
  draws the glyph on the map's payout and the wallet together.
- **The desktop shop scrolls inside the frame**, as above, pre-existing.
- **Relic icons and the item sprites' box** are placeholders until 5.0/5.
