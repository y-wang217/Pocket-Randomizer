# V2 report: world chrome

Stage V2 of `docs/spec/gymrun-visual-identity-plan.md`, run unattended under
`docs/visual/OVERNIGHT.md`. Every checkpoint green; guarded screens equal the
baseline to the pixel; smoke passes.

## Morning decisions

1. **Category and effectiveness chips are neutral now.** The plan's rule is
   that type chips are the only coloured chips, so `PHYS`/`SPEC` lost the
   fighting and water hues V0 gave them and `2x`/`½x`/`0x` lost green and red.
   4.5 built the category colour as the at-a-glance read across four buttons.
   If that read mattered more than the rule, the two `--chip` lines are
   `.badge--cat-physical` and `.badge--cat-special` in `styles.css`, and the
   effectiveness ones sit beside them; the V5 prompt says the marker must be
   "not brighter", which a hue at the same lightness satisfies.
2. **Status chips are neutral too**, same rule. `BRN` is three letters.
3. **The seed stamp carries the bare seed.** `contentHash` is not built, so
   the `GYMRUN-xxxxxx-nnnnnnn` form does not exist yet. `formatSeedStamp` in
   `ui/stamps.ts` is the one function to grow when it does; the stamp copies
   exactly what the seed bar accepts, so what is copied can be pasted.
4. **No per-screen stamp drop was needed.** The sweep found nothing painted
   under any stamp on any screen (table below). The rule slot is documented
   in `styles.css` under "corner stamps" for the first screen that collides.
5. **Item icons need the network.** They are cells of Showdown's item sheet,
   fetched at render time as the plan's asset rule asks. This sandbox has no
   route to that host, so every screenshot here shows empty icon cells, and
   both browser bots ignore that one failed load. On a phone with a network
   they draw.
6. **The party's slot bar sits above the cards, not instead of them.** The
   cards carry the stats, moves and actions the plan does not move anywhere
   else; the hotbar is the collection view and each card wears its number.
   The map's party panel is unchanged: it is on a guarded screen and its
   cards are not the party management screen.
7. **No stat-stage chip renders yet.** Nothing on screen renders a stat stage
   as a chip today (the battle panel colours the number), and V5 is the stage
   that puts stage chips on the panel. `stageChip` exists in `ui/chip.ts`
   for it.

## V2.3 numbered slots

`ui/slots.ts`. The party screen wears a hotbar of `PARTY_SIZE` slots above
its cards, the held item as an icon in its member's slot; the backpack wears
`tuning.backpackCapacity` slots above its rows. Every card and row carries
its number. Empty slots are dashed and empty. Nothing sorts;
`test/slots.test.ts` reorders the state and reads the slots back in the new
order, and checks a berry's slot has the classes a held item's has.

Icons resolve through `@pkmn/img`, new in `dependencies`, on its `adaptable`
entry with an index of GYMRUN's thirty-eight items generated by
`scripts/gen-item-icons.ts` into `src/ui/theme/itemIcons.ts`.
`test/item-icons.test.ts` regenerates the index against the full package and
fails on drift. The default entry would have cost 43 kB gzipped.
Screenshot: `v2-chrome/party-slots.png`.

## V2.5 chips

`ui/chip.ts` builds every small label; `test/chip.test.ts` scans `src/ui`
for one built by hand. One recipe on `.chip`; every variant keeps the class
it had (`type type--fire`, `tier tier--hard`, `band band--3`,
`badge badge--status`, the gate's `node__gate-need`/`node__gate-band`), so
metrics, selectors and tests hold. `BAND n` renders where it did, on the
reward card by the move name. The gate's two chips keep the gate line's own
metrics (body face, `--fs-gate`, no vertical padding) because the event node
is on the guarded map. The browser test reads every visible chip on the map
and the battle and finds one neutral computed style and the type chips apart
from it. No Release A band-badge-per-surface test exists to run.
