## The battle backdrops, brought forward from 5.0/5

The author delivered all nine paintings inside this stage so the asset pipeline
could be judged against the real screen
([`../../spec/gymrun-stage5.0-battle-backdrops.md`](../../spec/gymrun-stage5.0-battle-backdrops.md)).
Every battle now stands on its region's art, or the gym's. Sheets:
[`5.0-stage2/backdrops-phone.png`](5.0-stage2/backdrops-phone.png) (all nine on a
phone) and [`5.0-stage2/backdrops-by-field.png`](5.0-stage2/backdrops-by-field.png)
(nine backdrops by ten field states: none, five weathers, four terrains).

**How they got in.** Native 224x136 (the author's call, over the spike's 216x170),
drawn at 2 CSS px an art pixel, bottom-centre, never stretched.
`scripts/visual/backdrops.py` converts each 1536x1024 painting: crop sky to
224:136, area-average down, quantise to 48 colours. 147,633 bytes for nine, one
fetched per fight.

**What the pipeline should change**, in the order it matters:

1. **Composition against the HP boxes.** The opponent's box covers the stage's
   upper left, about 60% of the width and 35% of the height, and that is where
   every painting puts its landmark: the gym's banners and torches, the ruins'
   arch, the city's towers, the cave's crystals. What the player sees is mostly
   ground plus a slice of the upper right. Ask for the landmark in the **upper
   right**, behind the opponent, and plain ground in the middle band.
2. **The lower left is behind the player's back sprite.** Busy foreground props
   there (crystals, pillars, rocks) compete with the sprite's silhouette. Ask
   for low detail there, and leave the lower right plain: the player's box
   covers it.
3. **Generate on the grid.** The paintings' pseudo-pixels are 6 to 7 px and do
   not align to anything, so the conversion has to average them and colours
   blur at edges. Ask for exactly 224x136, or an exact multiple (1792x1088 at
   8x) with 8px-aligned pixels, no anti-aliasing, a palette of 32 to 48.
4. **Aspect.** Ask for 224:136 (about 1.65:1) directly; 3:2 costs 91 rows of
   sky at the crop.
5. **The gym's emblem is a Poke Ball**, on the floor and every banner. The plan's
   wordmark rule is *"Original. No Poke Ball."*; the same caution applies here.
   It is in as delivered; replacing it is the author's call.

**Under the field states.** Every wash and tint is visible on every backdrop.
The weakest pairs are snow on summit and sun or sand on badlands, where the wash
is close to the art's own colour; the header's field glyph carries the state
either way, colour secondary (section 2).
