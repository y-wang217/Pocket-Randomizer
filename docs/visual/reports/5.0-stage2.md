## What was built, by ruling

- **D60, the scene backdrop.** The stage asks the manifest for
  `battle-backdrop:<locale>`, or `battle-backdrop:gym` at a gym, and records the
  key on `data-backdrop`. Every key is still a placeholder: the locale's own
  tint, or a gym tint of its own. A file, when the art pass drops one in, sits
  over the tint, so a missing or broken file shows the placeholder at the
  stage's size. The weather wash and terrain tint stay on the stage, over
  whichever backdrop it has. The World behind the frame is untouched.
- **Platforms.** A CSS ellipse under each sprite, outside the actor so a beat
  moves the Pokemon and not its ground.
- **D56, the HP box.** The Pokemon panel in a window, every fact kept: name,
  gender and level on the top line with the opponent's roster count opposite;
  the bar in a heavy frame; status and the HP number (both sides) on one line;
  then types, ability, item, volatiles and stat stages. Names wrap rather than
  truncate. The long press to the base stats is unchanged.
- **D57, the move button.** The full move card in a window, three rows where it
  was four: the name alone on the top line, the identity line with PP at its
  right edge, then the fact strip. The forecast edge keeps its colours.
- **D58, the header and the strip.** The header is one row above the stage:
  the node glyph (and a leader's name), the opponent, the AI tier, the field
  glyph, and **the turn header**, `Turn 4`, which section 6 has asked for since
  the bible's first revision and no build had drawn. The flag strip hangs
  directly under the stage, so a turn's outcome sits against the Pokemon it
  happened to and above the buttons for the next turn.
- **D59, Switch.** The bench is behind one button under the grid. Its label is
  what the bench's heading said (`Switch`, `Switch — blocked this turn`,
  `Choose who comes in`), and the heading is gone. A forced switch opens the
  bench by itself; any choice closes it. No Info button: the log keeps D26's
  handle and pull on the strip.
- **D62.** Nothing to build on this screen: the type is already the icon chip on
  the panel, the move card and the bench.
