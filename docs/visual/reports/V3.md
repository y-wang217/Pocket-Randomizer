# V3 report: scene layer

Stage V3 of `docs/spec/gymrun-visual-identity-plan.md`, run unattended under
`docs/visual/OVERNIGHT.md`. Every checkpoint green; guarded screens equal the
baseline to the pixel; the performance gate passes on every locale without
the canvas fallback.

## Morning decisions

1. **Verify on a real phone; the 4x throttled trace is a proxy.** The plan's
   gate is a Chrome trace on a mid-range Android. What ran here is headless
   Chromium at 390x844 with the CPU throttled 4x, and the metric is the main
   thread's work per frame, not the interval between frames, because headless
   Chromium draws only when something changes and the interval is the wheel
   cadence. Every locale's p95 sits under 8.7ms against the 16ms gate, which
   is margin, but it is margin on a desktop core. `scripts/visual/perf.mjs`
   is the instrument; the numbers are below.
2. **The container is `.world`, not `.scene`.** `.scene` is the battlefield in
   this repo (`createScene` in `ui/scene.ts`, its rules in `styles.css`), and
   the first V2 cut showed what a shared class does. The world scene lives in
   `scene.ts` as the plan asks; only the class differs.
3. **The world follows `<html data-locale>`**, through a MutationObserver,
   rather than an app call. One writer for the region (`theme/locale.ts`),
   and any instrument that re-tags the attribute re-tags the world, which is
   how every per-locale screenshot and contrast reading here was taken.
4. **The near layer moves at 1.0 of scroll, literally.** So a foreground
   silhouette scrolls off the top of a long map with the page, as the plan's
   ratio says. If that reads as the world leaving rather than as depth, the
   ratios are `PARALLAX` in `ui/scene.ts`.
5. **The scrim is one number, 0.35, for every locale.** No text style fell
   below the V1 rule on any scene, so no locale needed its own. The token is
   `--scrim-opacity` in `tokens.css`, and a per-locale override belongs in
   `styles.css`, not `locales.css`.

## V3.1 performance baseline

`docs/visual/reports/v3-perf-baseline.json`, the V2 tree with the V1
gradient and no scene DOM. Method in decision 1. p95 main-thread work per
frame 5.81ms, mean 1.07ms; mean paint 0.2ms, p95 1.14ms; 101 frames over two
seconds of wheel scrolling.

## V3.2 the container

`createWorldScene()` in `ui/scene.ts`: `.world`, fixed, `inset: 0`,
`z-index: 0` under the shell's 1, `overflow: hidden`, `pointer-events: none`,
three `.world__layer` children (far, mid, near) and a `.world__scrim`.
Mounted once by `app.ts` beside the shell. Hidden with nothing in it until a
region arrives; empty and hidden again on the summary.

Tests: three layers and a scrim on mount; every locale fills all three and
mounts one drift; the same node on every screen of a SMOKE24 run by
`evaluateHandle` identity; every visible button, role=button and input on
every screen takes a hit-test at a point inside its visible box.

## V3.3 eight places

`src/ui/theme/scenes/<locale>.ts`, each three SVG strings on a 96x48 grid
plus a drift, `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMax slice"`, `shape-rendering:
crispEdges`. Every main shape is `fill="var(--layer-fill)"`; highlights are
`fill="var(--locale-glow)"`; the test rejects any other fill, any `<image>`,
`href`, `url(` or `<text>`. Flat generic silhouettes: stalactites and rubble,
a sea horizon and headland, peaks and a snowline, a block skyline and wires,
a canopy and trunks, columns and an arch, a bank and reeds, mesas and rock
stacks. No Pokemon, no Pokeball, no landmark.

## V3.4 motion

Parallax from one passive scroll listener: `translate3d(0, -scrollY x f, 0)`
with f = 0.2, 0.5, 1 on far, mid, near. Asserted in jsdom and as computed
matrices in Chromium. The drift is a `.world__drift` on the mid layer on a
32s linear loop (`--drift-period`) from off the left edge to off the right,
low in the frame. Under reduced motion it is not mounted, and the listener
does nothing; asserted in jsdom with a mocked `matchMedia` and in Chromium
with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` (no drift element, all three layers
`transform: none` after a scroll).
