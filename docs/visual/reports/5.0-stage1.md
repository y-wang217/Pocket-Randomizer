## What was built

**Density removal (`51557af`).**
- Every Pocket rule is now unconditional. Where a Pocket rule used to win only on the
  (0,2,0) its prefix carried, its value was folded into the base rule it beat.
- **Verified before touching anything else:** the gallery was built before and after,
  and the computed style of every element was diffed on all twenty surfaces, loaded and
  walked (about 50,000 elements). Nothing differed except the drawer's height, which
  lost the picker.
- The setting, the picker, `theme/density.ts` and `data/densityTuning.ts` are deleted,
  along with the content-hash exclusion. The hash is unchanged.
- The census, the heights instrument and the test harness lost their density axis.

**The shell (`e96fed7`, `4bd5310`).**
- **The frame.** A portrait column, at most 480px wide.
  - It is a CSS size container, so every narrow-screen rule asks the frame, not the
    viewport.
  - It is one viewport tall. Screens scroll inside it and the page never does.
  - The corner stamps became a status strip at its foot.
- **The nav.** Map, Team, Bag, Run Info, Settings. Each is one word plus a placeholder
  icon from the asset manifest.
- **The guard (D53, confirmed).** Over a pending decision, a tab opens a readout:
  - Team and Bag open the party drawer, which gained a read-only bag.
  - Map opens the map without its picker.
  - Run Info and Settings open their own screens.
  - Each fills the frame under the nav, and closing it returns to the decision.
  - Team and Bag open the writable party screen only from the map and pre-gym, where
    Manage already led.
  - `test/visual-shell-nav.test.ts` presses every tab over a fight and over the map, and
    holds the screen and the saved log byte for byte.
- **Run Progress, the decision feed (D55).**
  - `ui/decision-feed.ts` wraps the run policy outside the replay. Each logged decision
    becomes one line when `onDecision` reports it.
  - So there is one line per log entry, in log order, rebuilt on a resume, with nothing
    persisted.
  - `test/decision-feed.test.ts` holds three things:
    - the log is byte-identical with and without the wrapper;
    - the feed has one named line per decision;
    - a resume from any save gives the same lines.
- **Run Info.** A read-only screen holding:
  - where the run is (the map's own heading and rail);
  - the seed and the build;
  - the whole feed, newest first, grouped by gym.
- **Settings.** The battle speed picker, moved out of the drawer, and the tutorial replay.
- **The desktop sidebar**, at 1024px and wider:
  - where the run is;
  - the team, as `ui/slots.ts`'s hotbar at the run's live capacity;
  - the feed.
- **The palette.** The four V0 base values turned over to a light face, plus the plan's
  window tokens: heavy border, highlight, selected, disabled.
  - No gradients, no shadows and no `backdrop-filter` inside the frame.
  - The world still draws behind the frame on a desktop.
- **The battle stage** carries the plan's placeholder backdrop (a flat locale tint).
  The 4.11 weather wash and terrain tint moved onto it.
- **The asset manifest** (`ui/assets/manifest.ts`). Every class C key is present as a
  placeholder, at the 5.0/0 native sizes. A swap is one entry plus a file.

## Known gaps, on purpose

The author asked for this stage not to chase details the next stages reshape. So these
are left, each on a screen a later stage rebuilds:

- **The wide layouts** (over 760px) are unreachable inside a 480px frame, and dead.
  Each of 5.0/2 to 5.0/4 deletes its own.
- **The two-form copy markup.** Long and short spans, and word labels, are still
  rendered and hidden by CSS. Collapsing `Prose` to one string is a copy change, left
  for the stage that touches each screen.
- **The header row** (title, tutorial, seed) stays under the nav. The reference has a
  wordmark in the nav and no header. That comes with the art pass and a home for the
  seed controls.
- **The battle stage** is a placeholder tint with the old panel layout on it. It is
  5.0/2's whole job.
- **Placeholder letters** in the nav are counted by the census as words (M, T, B, R, S).
  Real icons remove them.
- **The currency glyph** is ruled (D54) and not drawn yet. It lands with the node card
  in 5.0/4.
