## What was built, by ruling

- **D63.** The step being chosen from carries the whole map node card: the
  mark in a highlighted disc, the tier pips and capability beneath, and the
  detail line (the payout as the currency mark and a number, the AI tier, a
  shop's shelf as a count and a coin amount; no kind hint, by D77). Every other row carries the mark, the
  pips and the capability; the rest of that node's card is on its mark's long
  press, composed from the same functions the face uses. A walked node's press
  says what it was.
- **D64.** The chevron keeps none, latent and known, on every row, from
  `resolveCapability`.
- **D75.** A node's place is its option index against a slot grid
  (`ui/map-layout.ts`), leaning by step parity. No hash. Per-backdrop grids are
  an empty table until the art pass.
- **D61.** The existing SVG marks. Trainer sprite for the player only.
- **D72.** The locale card's swatch is a strip of the region's map backdrop,
  through the same manifest key the graph uses. The gym stays in the rail.
- **The team off the map.** The party HUD and Manage are gone; the Team tab
  opens the same writable party screen. The wallet stays, on the heading's
  first line, as the currency mark.
- **D54, carried.** The currency family's one mark, drawn for the first time,
  on the payout and the wallet. Thirteen glyph families.
- **Commit behaviour, as the prompt asked to report:** unchanged. One tap on a
  current-step node commits through `nodePick.submit`, no confirm. Long press
  inspects any node.

## For the reviewer

- **WebKit and a real iPhone are the reviewer's**, as at 5.0/1 and 5.0/2. The
  graph leans on CSS size containers for the pitch floor (Safari 16+).
- **The desktop sidebar's team shows species and items, not HP.** With the team
  off the map, HP and status are one tap away on the Team tab on every
  viewport. Whether the sidebar should carry HP is a question for 5.0/3's
  sidebar pass, not a change made here.
- **All eight map backdrops are art**, delivered with this stage. Native
  size 272x408, 2:3 as painted; on a phone the drawing is
  lowered up to 88px so its entrance building sits at the entrance row. Every
  node stands in the painted clearing on the default slot grid, so
  `BACKDROP_GRIDS` is still empty. §89 items 19 to 21.
