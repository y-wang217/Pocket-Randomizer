## What was found, in one paragraph

The weather logic is `@pkmn/sim`'s and has run in every fight since Stage 0.
The tree reads it as an event only: `core/battle/flags.ts` emits a `field` flag
when weather or terrain starts, the strip prints *Rain*, and `mark-sweep` runs
inside the causing actor's box. Nothing carries the state: `BattleFacts` has no
field, `battle.field.weather` is never read, and the world behind the battle
(`createWorldScene`, keyed by `data-locale`) knows nothing about the fight.
Every weather in this game is set by an ability, because `gen-pools.ts`
excludes the setter moves on purpose, and abilities fire on switch-in, which is
the one protocol batch `screens/battle.ts` shows with `animate=false`. So the
commonest weather start is invisible outside the log, and the setter never gets
an `ability` flag because the reader drops the `[from]` tag.

The census ran and the rows are ruled. Four things differ from the plan as
filed, none of them by editing it:

1. **Nine marks, not eight**: Strong winds is its own glyph, because the primal
   weathers are half of all weather.
2. **The `ability` flag gains a second pattern** at Tier 4: `-activate` lines
   naming an ability, 7.2% of battles, no new kind.
3. **The move half of Tier 4 is not built**: `-activate|move:` is the trapping
   moves, 4.6%, already a volatile chip.
4. **Tier 2b, the field factor on the move button**, added by D49's ruling
   against the recommendation. C1 now has two exceptions. `CLAUDE.md` restates
   the old one and is the lead designer's to bring in line.
