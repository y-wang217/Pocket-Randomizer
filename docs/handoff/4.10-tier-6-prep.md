## The finding that reorders the tier

**On run one, the classroom is in Detailed, and Detailed paints no glyphs.**

Section 7 makes starter select the classroom where every glyph first carries
its label. `ui/density-guard.ts` forces Detailed on any screen with unseen coach
marks, and starter select has five. Walking every gallery surface for painted
`[data-family]` nodes:

| Surface | Pocket | Detailed |
|---|---|---|
| starter | stat 18, type 17 | **none** |
| battle | accuracy 3, category 4, pp 4, stat 3, type 17 | accuracy 3, stat 1 |
| result | category 1, pp 1, type 1 | **none** |
| map | capability 9, type 13 | capability 9 |
| summary | accuracy 8, category 24, pp 24, type 36 | accuracy 8 |

So on a first launch, the one moment the bible names for teaching the glyphs,
there are no glyphs on the screen to label. M6.1's done-when cannot be recorded
while the guard stands. **M6.2 deletes the guard.** This is **D43**, and its
recommendation is M6.2 → M6.3 → M6.1.

**And M6.2 is cheaper than it looks.** Every surface that carries marks, in
Pocket with the guard off, per anchor:

| Screen | Marks | Anchors painted in Detailed | Painted in Pocket |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| starter | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| locale | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| map | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| battle | 6 | 6 | 6 |
| result (reward + capture fixtures) | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| party | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| pre-gym | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| drawer | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| **total** | **29** | **29** | **29** |

**No mark would be silently dropped by deleting the guard today.** Tiers 2 to 5
took every fact that used to fold behind a tap in Pocket and put it on the
compact face, so the geometry the guard protected no longer differs. What M6.2
actually has to re-anchor is **the copy**.

## M6.1: four rows before it can be built

**D40. The classroom does not carry the families section 7 says it does.**
Starter select paints `stat` and `type` only, on all eight seeds tried.
`screens/starter-select.ts:98-113` draws each move as text — `90 BP`, `Status`,
`35 PP` — rather than mounting the move card, so category, band and PP never
appear. **34 of the screen's 51 Pocket words are `BP`, `PP`, `Status` and `HP`**,
field labels R2 forbids by name. Section 4 has no starter row and no tier ever
owned the screen. Recommendation: a new item, **M6.0**, that mounts the move card
there, as its own PR before M6.1.

**D41. Three of ten families never pass through the glyph.** Band pips
(`bandChip`), status chips (`statusChip`) and the effectiveness edge are drawn
without `glyphNode`, so they carry no `data-family` and a label *"rendered by
the glyph"* never fires for them. The store's unit test would pass for all ten
anyway. Recommendation: route them through `glyphNode`, plus a browser test that
walks every surface and asserts every painted family reports itself.

**D42. R2 and R3 outrank R7.** Section 1 says the lower-numbered rule wins, and
R3 forbids glyph plus name, which is what an exposure label is. Recommendation:
add R7's two exposures to R2's and R3's permits.

**D43.** Above. What counts as an exposure while Detailed paints words, and
the reorder.

**D44. The census has no exposure state.** The gallery runs on a fresh store,
so once labels render every surface counts them as words at rest.
Recommendation: pin the census to exhausted exposure and add a `first-run`
column, as one instrument commit before M6.1, the way D30 landed before M5.1.

**Two things M6.1 inherits that need no row.**

- **`data/glyphLabels.ts` does not move the hash.** D12 ruled it into
  `EXCLUDED` on the same basis as `data/glyphFamilies.ts`, and nothing under
  `core/` will import it.
- **The label copy half-exists already.** Every entry in `ui/theme/glyphs.ts`
  carries a `label` field documented as *"what the first-exposure label would
  say"*, and it is also the glyph's `aria-label`. M6.1 moves the per-family word
  to `data/glyphLabels.ts`; the per-glyph accessible name stays where it is.

**D39 is timed with M6.1** by its own recommendation (the Toll's `Costs`, option
2). It is still open; M6.1 either takes it or says why not.

## How to re-take the numbers

Both measurements were one throwaway browser test each against the gallery,
through `test/visual/harness.ts`, and deleted afterwards:

- **Families**: for each `GALLERY_SURFACES` entry and each density, count
  `[data-family]` nodes with no `[hidden]` ancestor and `checkVisibility()`
  true.
- **Anchors**: for each surface in `test/visual-tutorial-guard.test.ts`'s list,
  and each mark's selector in `TUTORIAL`, the same two checks — which is what
  `ui/tutorial.ts`'s `present()` does — with no `tutorial=fresh`, so the guard
  never runs.

D41's recommended test is the first of these made permanent.
