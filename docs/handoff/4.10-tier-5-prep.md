## The finding that reorders the tier

**Most of Tier 5's reduction is a render deletion, and a render deletion moves
no hash.** The locale blurb, the gym blurb, the map node's tier sentence and
the capability sentence are all data the screen chooses to print; not printing
them edits `ui/`, not `data/`. `contentHash` holds at `d4e080` through M5.2,
M5.3, M5.4 and M5.5 without anyone arranging for it to.

**Two items are the exception, and they are the two that rewrite copy.** M5.1
has to shorten thirty-eight item blurbs and ten relic descriptions inside two
hashed files; M5.6 has to reach the twenty-four events inside a third. Those
are D12's problem and D14's problem respectively, and D14 has been open since
Tier 0 waiting for exactly this.

So the tier's cost is concentrated in its first and last items, and the four in
the middle are cheaper than the census totals make them look.

## The measurements

Taken on this tree, at `8ecf208`, before any Tier 5 code exists.

### The events are not over budget by a margin. They are over by a factor.

| | |
|---|---:|
| Events over 40 words | **24 of 24** |
| Median event | 67 |
| Range | 62 to 75 |
| Hooks over 30 words | **0 of 24** |
| Labels over 6 words | **1 of 96** |

Every event meets every sub-budget section 4's own row names, and every event
fails the row's total. The overflow is entirely the four hints, at 8 to 15
words each — roughly 40 words per event before the hook is counted, and the
hints have no line in the budget at all. This is D33.

### The relic card's copy is two sentences, and the item's budget is one line.

| | entries | over 8 words | median | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `items.ts` `blurb` | 38 | 14 | 5 | 14 |
| `relics.ts` `playerDescription` | 10 | **8** | **16** | 18 |

Every relic description is the same shape: one sentence naming the capability,
one naming what the relic pays. *"Opens the way through anything overgrown.
Something turns up in the cleared brush after every fight."* Eight words cannot
hold both, and C2 says the one that leaves is re-encoded rather than dropped.
This is D34.

### The instrument grew a column, and the column changed the item.

D30 was filed as "add four selectors". Building it found that `renderTable`
**summed** every instance of a component, where D2's ruling says per component
*instance* and D1 makes every section 4 figure a ceiling. A ceiling binds the
worst instance; a sum cannot see it. Three reward cards at 22 is 8 + 8 + 6,
which passes, or 14 + 4 + 4, which does not. The table has a worst-instance
column now.

What it says, in Pocket, before anything is built:

| Component | worst | budget | over by |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| reward card | 8 | 8 | **0** |
| shop stock card | 11 | 8 | 3 |
| map node card | 12 | 0 | 12 |
| locale card | 6 | 0 | 6 |
| capture card, as a party row | 3 | 0 | 3 |

**M5.1's reward card is at its budget and M5.1 is not nearly done.** The
gallery's result screen offers an item, a heal and a TM; **no relic card
renders on any fixture in the tree**, and a relic description is 16 words at the
median. The heaviest reward card the instrument can see is not the heaviest one
the game draws, and the item either fixes the fixture or says the relic card was
asserted rather than censused. Third time for Tier 4's second finding.

**M5.2 is the largest single gap in the tier** — 12 against 0 on the worst node
card — and it is also the cheapest, because every word of it is render-side.

### One component is built twice, and one wears a class it does not mount.

`renderRewardCard`: **1** call site. `.shop__item`: its own 38 lines doing the
same job. The capture card: the party row's class, none of the party row's
code. Both in D29.

### The confirm overlay is built, and the census says it does not exist.

`scripts/visual/census.ts` prints `confirm overlay | absent | absent | absent`,
and the script documents `absent` as *"a component with no call site in the
tree yet"*. `ui/band.ts` has four call sites. What is absent is a fixture that
taps something. This is D31, and it is Tier 4's second finding in a new place:
**a fixture that cannot produce the condition an item exists for cannot measure
that item.**

## What D14 turns into

**D14 is no longer nobody's blocker.** It has been open since Tier 0 on the
strength that two words of flavour text were not worth a hash move. M5.6 does
not touch two words: whichever way D33 is ruled, the item reaches every hook,
label and hint in `data/events.ts` — to shorten them, or to stop rendering four
of them and delete the dead field.

D14's own recommendation already anticipated this: *"option 2, timed to
whenever something else moves the hash anyway, or taken on its own as a Tier 5
item beside M5.6."* That is now. Moving `hook`, `labels` and `hints` into
`data/eventCopy.ts` — which exists, is already in `EXCLUDED`, and is already
described as the event screen's copy — leaves `events.ts` holding `id`,
`locale`, `requires` and `toll`, which are the four fields a run actually
generates from. One hash move on the way through, and every future event
rewrite is free.

**It is the same trade D12 took and the opposite of what D14 has been holding
out for.** The reason to take it now rather than at Tier 0 is that Tier 0 was
buying two words and Tier 5 is buying the whole table.

## Three things the build should know before it starts

**1. Section 5's closing sentence is already true in the tree, in both of its
halves, on the surfaces this tier opens.** It reads: *"A component that exists
twice, or a screen that draws a stat without the stat block, is the defect this
document exists to prevent."*

*A component that exists twice.* `renderRewardCard` is exported from
`screens/reward.ts` and has **one** call site, `result.ts:200`.
`screens/shop.ts` never imports it — it builds `.shop__item` from scratch at
lines 86 to 123 with its own kind label, name, detail and price, and its own
`itemById`, `relicById`, `describeMove` and `moveCard` reads. Section 4 says the
shop card *"Follows the reward card"*. It does not. M5.1 is titled for both
surfaces and cannot hit one number on two pieces of code.

*A screen that draws a stat itself.* `locale-select.ts` draws species and level
into `.locale__party-member`; `run-map.ts` draws the same pair into the map rail
at line 633; `acquisition.ts:215` gives the capture card the party row's own
class and then hand-builds `panel__header` and `panel__meta` inside it at lines
218 to 266. The last of those is the worst kind, because it makes the census
charge the words to a component whose code never ran. Read
[`../design/inventory.md`](../design/inventory.md) §5 before drawing anything on
any of them.

**2. M5.5 is nearly built, and its item text is out of date twice over.** M2.3
shipped the replace band with both cards. M3.3 shipped the forfeit band —
*"Forfeit this reward?"*, `Forfeit`, `Keep` — with **one** card, and said why in
the file: *"a replace trades a move for a move and a decline gives one up for
nothing, and drawing a second card would be inventing a thing on the other side
of the trade."* D22 then moved the decline budget from 4 to 6. The record still
asks for *"the two cards"* and the milestone still says *"census reads 6 and
4"*. Per CLAUDE.md the record is not edited: this goes in `generation.md` as a
dated deviation. What is left of M5.5 is its last line — *"Overlay cancel and
flow decline visually distinct"* — plus D31's fixture.

**3. `blurb` is the field M5.1 asks for, under another name.** The item says to
add `playerDescription` to `data/items.ts` and `data/relics.ts`.
`relics.ts` already has it. `items.ts` has `blurb`, which
`screens/reward.ts` documents as having been that field since Stage 3. Nothing
needs adding; the fields need **moving**, per D12, and **shortening**, per D34.
Neither `blurb` nor `playerDescription` is read anywhere under `core/` — the
split is mechanical and the test will hold it.
